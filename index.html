Assessment & treatment

Hands-on assessment. Individual treatment.

Your appointment includes a detailed clinical history and a hands-on physical assessment of movement, joints, muscles, tendons, strength and relevant neurological function.

Treatment is tailored to the clinical findings and may include hands-on treatment and manual therapy where appropriate, alongside targeted exercise, rehabilitation, load-management advice and education.

The aim is not simply to treat the painful area, but to establish what is most likely driving your symptoms, explain the findings clearly and agree a practical plan to help you move with greater confidence.

About me

Nick Harris MSc, BSc (Hons), MCSP, HCPC

Advanced Physiotherapy Practitioner

BSc (Hons) Physiotherapy
Master's Degree (MSc) in Neuro-musculoskeletal Physiotherapy
HCPC Registered Physiotherapist
Member of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (MCSP)

I am an experienced musculoskeletal physiotherapist with nearly 20 years of clinical experience and almost 10 years working in advanced physiotherapy practice.

I initially qualified with a BSc (Hons) degree in Physiotherapy and subsequently undertook further postgraduate study, completing a Master's Degree (MSc) in Neuro-musculoskeletal Physiotherapy .

This advanced training has supported my development in the assessment, diagnosis and management of more complex musculoskeletal conditions.

Alongside Rebound, I continue to work within the NHS as an Advanced Physiotherapy Practitioner and First Contact Practitioner, assessing and managing a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions.

My particular clinical interests include spinal conditions, shoulder and upper-limb problems, and hip, knee and lower-limb conditions.

Outside the clinic

Outside physiotherapy, I enjoy keeping active through golf, football and general fitness. I also play guitar in a band.

I also volunteer as part of the medical team at Glastonbury Festival, working in the festival medical facilities and helping to assess and manage acute injuries and trauma.

My aim with Rebound is simple: to provide experienced, evidence-based musculoskeletal physiotherapy locally, with enough time to properly assess the problem, explain what is happening and agree a sensible plan for moving forward.

Privacy & professional information

Clear information about your care and your data.

Privacy notice

Rebound Advanced Physiotherapy Clinic is the data controller for personal information provided directly to the clinic.

This may include your name and contact details, appointment information and, when you become a patient, relevant health and clinical information required to provide physiotherapy care.

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange and manage appointments, provide and document physiotherapy care, communicate with other healthcare professionals where appropriate and meet professional or legal obligations.

Personal information is only shared where there is an appropriate reason to do so, for example with another healthcare professional involved in your care, where you have asked or agreed for information to be shared, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Questions concerning your information can be sent to reboundadvancedphysio@outlook.com .

This website currently has no patient contact form, online booking system, payment facility, advertising tracker or website analytics installed.
